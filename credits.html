3D anatomy

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Source: dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d.

The BodyParts3D models were modified for the app: selected, simplified, recombined into layers, repositioned into a shared coordinate frame and quantized.

Structures that BodyParts3D does not include were modeled by the app's authors and are released under the same license (CC BY 4.0): the six ear ossicles, the coccyx, the pharynx, thyroid and parathyroid glands, most of the spinal cord and the cauda equina, the muscles of mastication and several facial and abdominal-wall muscles and the latissimus dorsi, the cranial nerves I and V to XII, and the main nerves of the upper and lower limbs.

The visible body outline is based on the MakeHuman base mesh and rig, released into the public domain (CC0).
