Corpus: Anatomy Daily

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Corpus: Anatomy Daily ("Corpus", "the app", "we") is an anatomy study app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name or email, your study progress stays on your phone, and the information we do receive is anonymous.

Information stored only on your device

Your daily challenge results, streak, quiz counts, settings (such as the 3D background and reminder time) and similar progress are stored on your iPhone. We do not receive or back them up. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, hosted in the European Union. These events include:

  • which screens and features are used (for example, that a structure was viewed or a daily challenge was completed, with its score);
  • basic technical details: device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone;
  • a random identifier created when the app is installed, which is not linked to your name, Apple Account or any other identity;
  • crash and error reports (what went wrong in the app's code).

Your IP address is used by PostHog to estimate an approximate, country-level location. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA), we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share this information for advertising.

Subscriptions

Purchases of Corpus Pro are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To know whether Pro is active on your device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription history from Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone as a local notification. No notification data is sent to us, and you can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your result on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information is kept

Anonymous usage events and crash reports are kept for as long as they are useful to improve the app, and then deleted. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required to provide your subscription and for legal and accounting purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete information about you, and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect your name, email or account details, we usually cannot link the anonymous data to you. If you contact us, we will help as far as we can, for example by explaining how to reset your app data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
